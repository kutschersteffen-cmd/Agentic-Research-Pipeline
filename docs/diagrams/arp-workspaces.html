The five workspaces and what they hand each other

The app is organised as five workspaces, each running its own business processes. Processes cross boxes: a template ratified in R&D Lab scores an Argus run, Argus extracts the transition-plan indicators the Transition Intelligence Platform reads, and its alerts open engagements in StewardIQ. Data Hub sits underneath all four: its security master maps every security to one issuer, once, for the whole tool.
