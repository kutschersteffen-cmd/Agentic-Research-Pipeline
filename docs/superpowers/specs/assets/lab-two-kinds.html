THEMES / TOPICS: what is the world talking about and doing?

1 Detect

Emerging themes, issues and risks across a large portfolio. Says (calls), does (capex, revenue), market test (stock impact).

2 Define

Taxonomy of activities, direct and indirect along the value chain, each with an authoritative source. Sectors and industries.

3 Identify

Extraction theme (JSON) that finds companies by their specific business activities, with cited evidence.

4 Universe

Companies with verified exposure, reviewed and effective-dated. User-defined topics enter at step 2.

dashed link (guess): a theme can become a signal, e.g. theme attention

SIGNALS: can text predict returns, honestly?

1 Design

Idea from a paper or a hunch. Category, e.g. Sentiment and narrative momentum (STOXX paper, first member).

2 Generate

Score issuer text or search results. Grounded, versioned, point-in-time.
guess: stored-text and issuer-first search as comparable variants

3 Evidence

Rank-IC, post-cutoff split, costs, deflated Sharpe, PBO. Leakage and memory diagnostics reported as numbers.

4 Decide

Graduate (feeds scoring) or shelve with the evidence kept. Gate: GO / NO-GO / insufficient data.

Themes feed Score through the verified universe. Signals feed Score only after they pass the Evidence gate.
