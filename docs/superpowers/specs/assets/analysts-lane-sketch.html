Analysts lane: sketch

Equity analyst agents running sector rule packs. Daily batch. Internal use only.

Lower priority: after the Signals workbench, Themes gaps and shared stage

JOB 2: DAILY WATCH OF A UNIVERSE NO HUMAN CAN READ

1 Universe

Point-in-time list of names to watch. Each carries its sector, its rule pack and the date of its last scorecard.

2 Cheap daily screen

Filters, classifiers and embeddings over new filings and news for every name. No analyst agent yet. PARTIAL news classifier and feeds exist

3 Triage

Trigger or stale? Escalate only names that tripped a screen or whose scorecard is out of date. Daily cost cap applies. PARTIAL staleness check exists

4 Analyst agent

Reads the evidence against the sector rules. Every claim cites a source quote, or the rule is marked "not found". NEW

5 Scorecard + note

One row per rule: evidence, quote, score, confidence. The note is written only from scorecard lines. NEW

6 Alert

Event with cited evidence, materiality, and the scorecard lines it changed. NEW

7 Review queue

A person accepts or overrides. Overrides are recorded. Count shows in "needs you". EXISTS

8 Decide and use

Accepted scorecards feed shared scoring, or become a sixth signal category via the Signals workbench.

Job 1, on demand: a user picks a company and a rule pack and runs steps 4 to 7 directly, with no screen or triage.

Sector rule packs

  • User-written in the app, or agent-derived from authoritative sources (like taxonomy research).
  • Versioned and ratified before an agent may use them.
  • Rules run in the deterministic rules engine EXISTS (Decision Studio templates).

Evidence for the lane

  • Alert precision and recall on a labelled sample of real events.
  • False-alert rate, cost per name per day, lag from event to alert.
  • Citations verify, reruns agree, human analyst agreement on a sample.
  • Where a rating exists: how it did against later returns.
