# Start page (ARP)

Scope: the app's start page (`#/home`) and, through the shared tokens, the whole app's visual world. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience: stewardship/ESG team; screen-shared in committees, checked on phones. Job: see what waits on a person and open it. Constraint: every number traceable; agents propose, a named person ratifies.
Chosen direction: Countersign (security printing). Memorable moment: ratifying draws an engraved seal beside the signer's name.
Unresolved: seal animation placement beyond the ballot cast; how far the engraved band reaches on inner pages.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A figure is final only once countersigned. The start page is a register of processes under one factual certificate band, refusing the dashboard of icon cards, greetings and hero metrics.
OWN-WORLD: Intaglio green chrome and rules on a pale green-grey underprint; engraved capitals (Cinzel) for page titles only; Source Sans 3 for all read or operated text with tabular figures; stamp violet means waiting on a person; red means error; a black VOID stamp means a failed run; one guilloche rosette, only in the band and on seals.
STORY: The steward sees what is due and why, trusts each count because its parts are named, and opens the one row that needs them.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the green sidebar. Top of main, a bordered certificate band whose heading is the due sentence ("3 ballot items before Shell plc's AGM on 8 Oct, ..."), rosette at its right edge, an as-of line under it. Below, the ruled register: serial, process, step marks, prepared by, countersign, open; the countersign column is the primary action.
FORM: Security printing (share certificate, banknote, registrar stamp); position 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 6e3f3e8c.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
