# Which visual fits which claim

- kpi_row: two to four headline numbers side by side, each a fact on its own.
- chart: a trend, ranking or split read from one dataset, with an optional stat card for the takeaway.
- comparison_table: options or items compared on the same few columns.
- two_column: two sides set against each other (before/after, us/them, risk/return).
- points: three to six reasons or facts that fit no other visual.

Emphasis: at most one per slide, on the thing the headline names.
