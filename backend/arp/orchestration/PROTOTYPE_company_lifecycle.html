How does the pipeline move a company?

The question this demo answers: a company goes identity → document discovery → extraction → review → data library (the "Onboard a portfolio" path). Does the order feel right when things go wrong? Specifically: can a company get stuck, can it skip a step, and what should happen when a new document appears after a company was already approved? (Today nothing marks the approved figures as out of date. This demo lets you choose.)
Assumptions: review is per company here (the real queue is per field); "edit" in identity review means a person typed the website/CIK; stages and checks mirror docs/PROCESS_VIEW.md, schemas/discovery.py and schemas/datapoints.py.
