TotalsCo plc Annual Report 2025

Group results
Group revenue was GBP 2.1 billion in 2025, compared with GBP 1.9 billion in 2024. Capital expenditure for the group was GBP 180 million. Research and development expenses for the group were GBP 25 million.

Our business
TotalsCo supplies industrial pumps, valves and flow-control equipment to the water, oil and gas, and chemicals industries.
