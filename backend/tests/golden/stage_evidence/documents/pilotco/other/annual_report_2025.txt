PilotCo AG Annual Report 2025

Business overview
PilotCo designs and manufactures alkaline electrolysers for industrial hydrogen production. Our alkaline electrolyser product line has been in series production since 2021, and we delivered systems with a combined capacity of 180 MW to customers in 2025.

Next generation technology
In March 2025 we commissioned a 5 MW pilot plant for our next-generation anion exchange membrane (AEM) electrolyser stack at our Dresden site. The pilot plant will validate the AEM stack design before a decision on pre-series production in 2027.

Financial review
Group revenue was EUR 412 million in 2025. Research and development expenses were EUR 38 million.
